How do I know if I'm ready to buy a home?

You're ready when you have stable income, savings for a down payment, and a clear idea of your future plans.

What's the best time of year to sell my home?

Spring and early summer are often popular times for selling properties.

How much should I offer on a house?

Your offer depends on the property's value, condition, and the local market.

What are closing costs and who pays them?

Closing costs are additional expenses involved in completing a property transaction.
